## 2. Presentation

The subject addresses the graphic and audiovisual representation of fashion ideas through advanced digital techniques. Students learn to design brand images for fashion labels, create digital campaigns, and manage proprietary and generic digital file formats. Competencies develop in photo retouching, avatar creation, video editing, web layout and digital portfolio, and in designing digital fashion experiences, including holograms and augmented reality. The approach fosters creativity and the ability to solve visual-communication challenges in the fashion business context.

## 3. Competencies

- CON1. Distinguish the different audiovisual representation techniques for fashion ideas and their possibilities and dissemination channels.
- HAB1. Represent fashion ideas graphically.
- HAB10. Design the brand image of a fashion label.
- COMP8. Ability to use digital graphic or audiovisual representation tools in communicating fashion ideas or projects, creating and improving corporate image and brand.
- COMP9. Ability to create new ideas and concepts from known ideas and concepts, reaching conclusions or solving problems, challenges and situations in an original way.

## 4. Learning outcomes

From PDF §3 (also in JSON `learning_outcomes`):

- Apply advanced computer tools in the creation and digital representation of fashion work.
- Produce digital fashion campaigns.
- Manage proprietary and generic digital file formats.
- Produce technical fashion drawings and videos.
- Layout design projects and work to professional quality.
- Design creative digital-communication strategies in fashion companies.
