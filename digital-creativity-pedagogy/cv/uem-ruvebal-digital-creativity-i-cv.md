## 2. Presentation

Digital Creativity I introduces students to audiovisual representation techniques applied to fashion ideas, exploring both three-dimensional form and its geometric representation. The subject covers the professional use of digital technologies for processing images, text and sound, as well as selecting vector-drawing and image-editing tools. Students develop competencies to communicate fashion projects through graphic and audiovisual resources, and to design a brand image. The course also fosters creativity and the ability to solve problems in original ways, using software and digital effects in the creation of animated images and digital still lifes.

## 3. Competencies

- CON1. Distinguish the different audiovisual representation techniques for fashion ideas and their possibilities and dissemination channels.
- HAB9. Use digital technology professionally for processing images, text and sound.
- HAB10. Design the brand image of a fashion label.
- COMP8. Ability to use digital graphic or audiovisual representation tools in communicating fashion ideas or projects, creating and improving corporate image and brand.
- COMP9. Ability to create new ideas and concepts from known ideas and concepts, reaching conclusions or solving problems, challenges and situations in an original way.

## 4. Learning outcomes

From PDF §3 (also in JSON `learning_outcomes`):

- Identify the fundamental concepts of three-dimensional form and its geometric representation.
- Recognise three-dimensional representation techniques.
- Distinguish the different technical resources for building digital three-dimensional models.
- Associate different effects software and digital image-editing resources.
- Use image and text in digital creations.
- Select vector-drawing tools applied to design and communication.
