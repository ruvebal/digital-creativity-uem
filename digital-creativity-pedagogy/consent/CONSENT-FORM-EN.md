## What is this study?

We want to understand whether students' ways of reading fashion images and
keeping process evidence change across the term, and whether the practice quizzes
and Lab traces help prepare for the knowledge-test instrument. Results would
evaluate a teaching innovation. They cannot prove that the course *caused* any
change. **No data are collected under the current draft.**

## What would I do? (only if the study is later approved)

- **Optional practice:** use the public practice quizzes (answers on click). Nothing
  on those pages is recorded.
- **If a pre/post classroom task is approved later:** short, timed image-reading or
  process-note tasks in week 1 and near term end; optional peer rating of anonymous
  process traces. Exact instruments stay unpublished until ethics approval.
- **Optional research reuse (C1):** after grades close, a third person may include
  pseudonymised portfolio traces you already submitted for assessment.

## Is it compulsory? Does it affect my mark?

No. Taking part is voluntary. Saying no, or stopping at any time, has **no
effect on any mark** in this or any other course. Practice quizzes are never
graded. Consent for research reuse is **grade-neutral** and not required to pass
Creación Digital (GDPR Art. 7(4) course rule — see `consent/README.md`).

## How is my information protected?

- You use a **code you make up yourself** for any study sheets, never your name or
  student number, if instruments are approved later.
- Results are reported only as group totals; no group smaller than five people
  is shown.
- Your data would be processed on the basis of your consent, under the EU GDPR
  and Spanish data-protection law. You can ask the data-protection contact above
  about your rights.

## Can I withdraw?

Yes — once a live study exists, give your code to [third person] by [date] and
your answers are deleted. After codes are dropped, data can no longer be linked.

## Risks and benefits

No known risks beyond time. No direct benefit; practice quizzes may help you
prepare for knowledge tests without being the test.
