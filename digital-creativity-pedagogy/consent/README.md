## Law vs course rule

**In force:** Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), applicable since 25 May 2018. Official text: [CELEX 32016R0679](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679).

Operative clauses used here (not a substitute for DPO advice):

- **Art. 6(1)(a)** — processing is lawful if “the data subject has given consent to the processing of his or her personal data for one or more specific purposes.”
- **Art. 7(2)** — the request for consent must be “clearly distinguishable from the other matters,” in “clear and plain language.”
- **Art. 7(3)** — withdrawal at any time; “as easy to withdraw as to give consent.”
- **Art. 7(4)** — when judging whether consent is freely given, “utmost account shall be taken of whether … the performance of a contract, including the provision of a service, is conditional on consent to the processing of personal data that is not necessary for the performance of that contract.”

**Course rule (exceeds the statutory minimum for teaching):** research reuse and exhibition are **opt-in**, **grade-neutral**, and **not required to pass** Creación Digital. Art. 7(4) is why: the “service” here is the assessed course. Making a mark depend on research or showroom consent would poison freely given consent.

**Not claimed:** that Art. 6(1)(e) (public task) is unavailable for ordinary teaching records. The DPO may choose a different lawful basis for the *gradebook*. This pack only governs **research reuse** and **public display**, which we treat as Art. 6(1)(a) until the DPO says otherwise.

**Spanish implementing law** (LOPDGDD) and UEM’s own DPO procedure apply on top. Do not invent article numbers here; the DPO fills them.

**Minors:** undergraduate groups may include 17-year-olds. Flag to the DPO; do not collect C/S until that check is done.

## Non-negotiable safeguards (same as FE §6)

1. **IP (or designated third party) administers C.** The teacher does not know who opted in until grades are closed.
2. **Temporal blindness:** no research analysis before the actas of that course are closed.
3. **Pseudonymisation at source**; correspondence table not held by the teacher.
4. **Revocable** without academic consequence.
5. **S1–S5 are per artefact**, not a blanket “the course may exhibit everything.”
6. **vShowroom v1** stores pieces + designer identity only if S1+S2 are signed; collect/follow stays client-side unless accounts are later justified (synergy §5).
