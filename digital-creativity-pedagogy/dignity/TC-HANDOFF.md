## Proposed TC research questions

1. **Problem framing:** whose dignity is absent from the initial creative
   brief, and who has the authority to define the problem?
2. **Divergence:** when does rapid ideation reproduce an available stereotype,
   and what source-informed constraints open other directions?
3. **Selection:** do novelty, marketability and humour suppress objections
   from people with less authority in the group?
4. **Co-creation:** when is participation substantive, and when does an
   activity extract stories, images or emotional labour?
5. **Creative labour:** does the romance of creativity hide exploitation or
   uneven recognition of group members' contributions?

## Activity prototypes for the next forge

| Prototype | Artefact | Critical test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reframe a fictional brief from three affected positions | Three problem statements and a disagreement log | Are invented role assumptions mistaken for actual participant testimony? |
| Generate alternatives under an agency constraint | Idea set plus selection reasons | Does the constraint create options or simply censor an inconvenient question? |
| Minority-report selection panel | Chosen concept with strongest rejected objection | Can a dissenting reading change the selection criteria? |
| Repair the method | Revised ideation/facilitation protocol | Can participants refuse disclosure or withdraw an idea without penalty? |
| Contribution and credit map | Group process record | Is visibility mistaken for the amount or value of work? |
