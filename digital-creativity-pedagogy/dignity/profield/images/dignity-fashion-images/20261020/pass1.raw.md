## Documented execution decisions

- [ESTABLISHED] V01: The ASA upheld an objection to Boohoo’s particular T-shirt product images on objectification and sexualisation grounds on 16 February 2022. The execution was seen in November 2021. Source: [Advertising Standards Authority. 2022. ASA Ruling on Boohoo.com UK Ltd.](https://www.asa.org.uk/rulings/boohoo-com-uk-ltd-a21-1136109-boohoo-com-uk-ltd.html)

- [ESTABLISHED] V02: The 6 April 2016 Gucci ruling distinguished two images and found one irresponsible in its presentation of thinness. It does not diagnose the model’s health. Source: [Advertising Standards Authority. 2016. ASA Ruling on Guccio Gucci SpA.](https://www.asa.org.uk/rulings/guccio-gucci-spa-a15-321743.html)

- [ESTABLISHED] V03: The revised 6 March 2024 Calvin Klein ruling reversed the objectification finding about FKA twigs while maintaining the objection to untargeted placement. Source: [Advertising Standards Authority. 2024. ASA Ruling on Calvin Klein Inc, revised.](https://www.asa.org.uk/rulings/calvin-klein-inc-g23-1194611-calvin-klein-inc-1.html)

## Claims, narrative and circulation

- [ESTABLISHED] V04: ACM secured commitments from H&M and Decathlon in 2022 to adjust or stop insufficiently clear sustainability claims. Commitments must not be described as fines or criminal convictions. Source: [Netherlands Authority for Consumers and Markets. 2022. Going forward, Decathlon and H&M will provide better information about sustainability to consumers.](https://www.acm.nl/en/publications/going-forward-decathlon-and-handm-will-provide-better-information-about-sustainability-consumers)

- [EMERGING] V05: Pantano’s study examines the Dolce & Gabbana chopsticks controversy through negative stereotype adoption and online diffusion. It supports a situated narrative and circulation analysis, not an adjudicated universal racial-discrimination finding. Source: [Pantano, Eleonora. 2021. When a luxury brand bursts. Journal of Business Research 123:117–125.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbusres.2020.09.049)
