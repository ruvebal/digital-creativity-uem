# Dignity cascade — master plan

Date: 2026-10-20. Branch: `codex/dignity-cascade`.
Scope: five supplied Dignity PDFs; existing critical-thinking/ethics vaults;
two distinct Profield investigations (ideas and images); a public `/dignity/`
student guide with image and media pathways; a later TC handoff.

Execution result and review packet: [`STATUS.md`](STATUS.md).

## Research questions

1. What does the supplied corpus mean by dignity, and what practical
   facilitation conditions does it require?
2. How do critical thinking, recognition, agency, objectification, labour,
   identity and platform circulation connect dignity to fashion communication?
3. Which documented campaign decisions can students inspect, challenge and
   redesign without reproducing humiliation or flattening differences?
4. What evidence should an image exercise and a media exercise produce?

| D6 | `/dignity/` guide and activity proposals | Skill-led structure and prose; bibliography; image/media pathways; assessable artefacts; voluntary participation |
| D7 | Build, publication/citation/campaign gates and critical review | No private metadata in HTML; links and bibliography checked; unresolved limitations reported |
| D8 | TC handoff | Transfer questions and design proposals only; TC curriculum changes remain a later stage |

D3a and D3b start as independent subprocesses before the final synthesis.
External research uses public thematic keywords; supplied PDFs and private
vault passages are not uploaded to external research services. The same agent
may prepare research and audit it, but that audit must not be described as an
independent reviewer or independent model pass.

## Publication and classroom decisions

- One supplementary guide, not a change to official hours, weights or outcomes.
- Root route `/dignity/`, English-first, with links to existing image and media lessons.
- Existing allocated NM campaigns stay in their sole lesson; the guide links to
  those lesson sections instead of reproducing their campaign analyses.
- Additional cases receive stable IDs and explicit supplemental/unranked status.
- “Violates dignity” is a reasoned ethical conclusion requiring evidence;
  regulatory findings are stated only in their exact jurisdiction and scope.
- Do not equate sexual expression with objectification, diversity with inclusion,
  provocation with harm, or a withdrawn advertisement with an adjudicated violation.
- Students can use a text-only case or fictional brief; no personal testimony,
  public posting, body comparison or collection of sensitive experiences required.
- Full corpus provenance stays private; public prose uses complete bibliographic
  entries and clear transfer boundaries. No private source PDFs are republished.
