# Canvas activity: Reading dignity in a fashion campaign

**Canvas item:** Assignment (text entry or file upload)  
**Use:** Formative, ungraded analysis; it does not change the course assessment requirements.  
**Suggested time:** 35–45 minutes  
**Submission:** 400–600 words, or an accessible equivalent such as a one-page annotated storyboard.

## Student prompt

Choose one case below. Study the linked regulator record or research source before deciding what you think. Some original campaign assets have been withdrawn or are not preserved on an authoritative public page. When an original is unavailable, analyze the regulator's description or scholarly account and say so; do not present a repost as an authenticated original. Do not download or redistribute sensitive campaign images.

Submit a short analysis with these headings:

1. **Case and evidence.** Identify the campaign or execution, source, date/version, medium and one documented finding. Link the exact source you used. If you analyze video, give a timestamp from the version you watched.
2. **Reading of the image or media.** Point to one specific choice of framing, pose, styling, voice-over, text, product claim, placement or circulation. Explain how it shapes who appears to act, speak, belong, or receive value.
3. **Dignity question.** Explain a plausible dignity concern and a reasonable alternative reading. Distinguish your interpretation from what the source establishes. Do not infer a person's identity, consent, health, intention or working conditions from appearance alone.
4. **Revision.** Propose one image or media change and name what further evidence would be needed to judge whether the change improves representation or accountability.

There is no required moral verdict. The work is assessed formatively for traceable evidence, careful reasoning, a serious alternative reading and a specific revision proposal.

## Case bank: direct sources

- **Boohoo, T-shirt product listing (2021):** [ASA ruling and detailed description of the three product images](https://www.asa.org.uk/rulings/boohoo-com-uk-ltd-a21-1136109-boohoo-com-uk-ltd.html). The ASA says Boohoo removed the images; use the ruling's description rather than treating an unverified repost as the original.
- **Gucci Cruise 2016:** [ASA ruling on the still images at the end of a video](https://www.asa.org.uk/rulings/guccio-gucci-spa-a15-321743.html) · [official Gucci short film](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EvcdVVHta1U). The ruling concerns one ending still and distinguishes it from another; the video is context, not proof that every copy contains the exact adjudicated frame.
- **H&M Conscious sustainability wording:** [Netherlands ACM case record](https://www.acm.nl/en/publications/going-forward-decathlon-and-handm-will-provide-better-information-about-sustainability-consumers). ACM reports commitments to clarify or stop using some claims, not a fine or criminal conviction. This is a claim and accountability case; the page does not preserve a complete campaign execution.
- **Dolce & Gabbana, “Eating with Chopsticks” (2018):** [peer-reviewed analysis in *Asia*](https://doi.org/10.1515/asia-2021-0017) · [contemporaneous report reproducing the campaign post](https://www.thedrum.com/news/dolce-gabbana-slammed-once-again-its-latest-dg-loves-china-campaign). The original social posts were deleted; compare source descriptions and distinguish the campaign video from later apology posts and commentary.
- **Calvin Klein / FKA twigs and Kendall Jenner (2023 posters):** [revised ASA ruling, with descriptions of all three ads](https://www.asa.org.uk/rulings/calvin-klein-inc-g23-1194611-calvin-klein-inc-1.html) · [ASA explanation of why it revised its ruling](https://www.asa.org.uk/news/revised-calvin-klein-ruling.html). The revised decision reversed the objectification finding for FKA twigs but maintained an objection to placement in an untargeted medium; retain that distinction.

## Canvas setup notes for the instructor

Create a Canvas Assignment with **Online submission → Text entry** and optionally **File Uploads**. Set it to ungraded/formative, or use the local course's existing formative category if one applies. Keep submissions private to the student and teaching team. The assignment instructions above can be pasted into the Canvas description; the direct-source list can be added as links. Canvas then stores each student's submission and feedback in the course. Do not collect answers through a public GitHub Pages form or issue tracker.

This repository has no connected Canvas publishing tool, so this file is a paste-ready activity rather than an item already created in the live LMS.
