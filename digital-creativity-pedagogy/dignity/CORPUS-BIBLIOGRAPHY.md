# Supplied Dignity corpus — bibliography audit

Audited 2026-10-20. Titles and attributions reflect the documents, not PDF
filenames or an LLM's inferred headings. `n.d.` below records an unknown
publication date; the current citation resolver cannot treat those records
as ordinary evaluator-safe author-year citations. Full correction receipts
and prior values are preserved in `bibliographic-corrections.json`.

1. Global Dignity. n.d. *Dignidad: Lo esencial*. Spanish teaching presentation,
   20 PDF pages. The PDF creation timestamp is not promoted to publication year.
2. *Formación Embajadores y Embajadoras de la Dignidad: Cuéntanos tu experiencia*.
   n.d. One-page staff experience form. Intellectual author unverified. Its
   June 2027 event date does not date publication. No form submitted.
3. Global Dignity España and Fundación AYO. 2026. *Dignidad e innovación
   educativa: Banco de buenas prácticas*. 21 fichas, actualización junio 2026.
   22 PDF pages. Case GD16, by Belén María Moreno Cabezalí, is on PDF page 17.
4. Global Dignity. n.d. *Global Dignity: Red de Embajadores y Embajadoras de la
   Dignidad*. General presentation, 10 PDF pages. Historical dates in the body
   do not establish the edition's publication date.
5. Global Dignity. n.d. *Guía del facilitador: Workshop Dignity*. Spanish
   facilitator guide, 11 PDF pages. Template authorship metadata is not
   sufficient to identify the intellectual author or publication year.

All five originals were read, extracted, semantically enriched and indexed.
The 64-page corpus produced 772 nodes and 275 semantic annotations. The
semantic pass processed 314 eligible text nodes; null and unknown-vocabulary
outputs were retained as audit outcomes or rejected by the engine, rather
than converted into guessed concepts. The annotations are local-model
classifications, not manually verified statements about educational quality.

The public guide's other references are recorded in `EVIDENCE-MATRIX.md` and
the shared course bibliography. Original PDFs remain in the supplied folder
and the local research library; no source PDF is republished on the course site.
