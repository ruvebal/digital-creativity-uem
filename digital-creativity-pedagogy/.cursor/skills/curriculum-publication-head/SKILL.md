# Curriculum publication head

Read the current root's `AI-EVIDENCE-HARNESS.md` (or Profield's
`docs/AI-CURRICULUM-HARNESS.md`) and the field's FieldSpec before proposing
content changes.

1. Treat Profield as the evidence factory, not the long-lived authoring copy.
   Make approved CV, forge and lesson edits in the relevant curriculum root.
2. Do not treat a vector hit, copied chat citation, local-model verdict, or
   Claude audit as proof. Resolve the source in Ahmes and cite the safe
   node/page; preserve `[BIBLIO-GAP]` where that is not possible.
3. Keep HE research, practitioner evidence, and platform/vendor documentation
   visibly distinct in the resulting lesson. A phenomenon study does not prove
   a teaching sequence.
4. Before promotion, create/update `grounding/PROFIELD-SYNC.json` in the
   curriculum root with the Profield run ID, audited artifact hash, source
   manifest, evaluator-safe status, gaps, target files and release decision.
5. Do not publish automatically. Show a concise diff and wait for the content
   owner's approval when the change affects learning outcomes, assessment,
   bibliographic claims, or student-facing prose.
