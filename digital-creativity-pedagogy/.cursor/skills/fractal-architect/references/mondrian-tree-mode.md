## Formula disposition

`geometry_only`. Bountis et al. motivate *that* early trees admit fractal /
scaling readings; this mode does **not** ship transcribed fractal-dimension
equations as governing LaTeX until a separate formula-fidelity audit promotes
them. Public captions cite the paper as inspiration limits, not as copied
formulae.

Canonical geometry (inspectable in the generator):

- axiom: apex branch of unit length;
- rewrite: forward; branch ±θ; continue with length `/ R`;
- fixed envelope: `θ = 26°`, `R = 1.45`, depth `6` (title-safe left void);
- seed string: `mondrian-tree:<surface>:<family>`.
