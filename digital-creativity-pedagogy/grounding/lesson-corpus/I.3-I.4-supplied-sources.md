## I.3 · colour and bitmap images

### Balasubramanian 2026

The article frames fashion curriculum integration through TPACK and reports a
structured digital-fashion collection workflow using Firefly, Photoshop, and
Illustrator. It is useful for a process-led colour/image exercise, but it does
not validate this course’s sequence or prove that AI improves learning.

> “AI tools as a supporting tool rather than a replacement to human creativity.”

### Nobile et al. 2021

The review places colour forecasting, palette extraction, visual retrieval, and
generative image practices within the wider digital-fashion research field.
Use it to ask what a computational palette removes from a situated garment
reading, not to treat palette extraction as interpretation itself.
