# Omnibus Research Landscape Map — CANONICAL REFERENCE

## Digital creativity for fashion communication — image, motion, virtual fashion, and immersive brand media

_Last updated: 2026-08-08_ (pass-2 audit + pass-3 tool verify: precision fixes kept; Park/IASDR **restored** with DOI `10.21606/iasdr.2025.845`; several Pass-2 “unverifiable” items upgraded; remaining unresolved cluster left tagged)

## Evidence tags

- **[ESTABLISHED]** Strongly supported by standards, curriculum frameworks, canonical literature, or repeated empirical findings.
- **[EMERGING]** Supported by recent 2024–2026 studies, preprints, working groups, or early empirical work, but not yet settled.
- **[UNVERIFIED]** Plausible field claim, but no sufficiently checkable source found in this pass — or Pass 2 could not corroborate the cited item.
- **Blank to update** = do not infer; rerun later.

### Twin mandate (durable vs frontier)

The **durable layer** is visual/media literacy: colour interaction, composition, photography, art direction, timing, narrative construction, garment/form knowledge, critical studio practice and coherent identity. The **fast-moving 2024–2026 layer** is controllable GenAI, diffusion-based VTON, multi-view/video garments, avatar/digital-human systems, XR/volumetric fashion media and AI-mediated studio practice.

# 0. Executive synthesis

**[ESTABLISHED]** Fashion communication is best treated as a **transmedia visual-discursive system** (shows, film, editorial, social, web, immersive) rather than as advertising alone. FACTUM 2025 work (Linfante; Zanola et al.) and earlier fashion-film scholarship support this framing.

**[ESTABLISHED]** Durable creative fundamentals remain tool-independent: colour interaction (Albers; Itten), fashion photographic literacy (Shinkle), animation principles (Thomas & Johnston), and garment/form knowledge underneath virtual-fashion CAD.

**[ESTABLISHED]** “Digital fashion” is a mature enough category for systematic reviews (Nobile et al. 2021; Baek et al. 2022), while diffusion-based VTON (IDM-VTON, CatVTON, StableVITON, FitDiT) is a major 2024–2026 computer-vision frontier.

**[EMERGING]** GenAI-in-studio fashion education is a recognisable but young research area (Anwar et al. 2025 SLR; Rizzi & Bertola 2025 Artificial A(i)rchive; Balasubramanian 2026 TPACK curriculum paper). Authorship, process transparency and assessment are the live pedagogical problems.

**[ESTABLISHED]** AR virtual try-on is the strongest immersive research strand; “holographic fashion” as peer-reviewed theory is thin, though historical Pepper’s-ghost deployments (notably McQueen A/W 2006) are well documented.

**[UNVERIFIED / GAP]** Fashion-specific motion-graphics evaluation frameworks and unified portfolio UX ↔ hiring-outcome models remain genuine blanks; adjacent UX/animation literatures can be imported but are not substitutes.

# 2. Digital fashion image making, colour, composition, and photographic retouching

## 2.1 Fundamentals

- [ESTABLISHED] **FUNDAMENTAL — colour relationships, hierarchy, composition, figure/ground, light, framing and perceptual organisation remain tool-independent knowledge.** Sources: Josef Albers, _Interaction of Color_, Yale University Press, 1963; Johannes Itten, _The Art of Color_ / _Kunst der Farbe_, 1961.

- [ESTABLISHED] **FUNDAMENTAL — fashion photography is not simply product representation; it is an image-making system through which bodies, garments, desire and cultural identity are constructed.** Source: Eugénie Shinkle, ed., _Fashion as Photograph: Viewing and Reviewing Images of Fashion_, I.B. Tauris/Bloomsbury, 2008, ISBN `9781845115173`.

## 2.2 State of the art / frontier

- [ESTABLISHED] **STATE OF ART — skilled digital image production remains a sequence of aesthetic decisions rather than a purely technical post-production operation.** Source: Ida Roivainen, “‘How I edit my Instagram images’: investigating skilled …,” _Visual Studies_, pp. 569–585, online **6 June 2024**, DOI `10.1080/1472586X.2024.2353689`. _(Pass-2 fix: online 2024, not “2025”; studies YouTube/Instagram lifestyle creators — adjacent to, not specific to, fashion.)_

- [ESTABLISHED] **STATE OF ART — professional retouching should be treated as a socio-technical image practice with ethical expertise, not merely Photoshop technique.** Source: Matthew Raj Webb, “Compositing the body: Ethical expertise among … professional photographic retouchers,” _Visual Anthropology Review_ 42(1):e70002, **2026**, DOI `10.1111/var.70002`. _(Pass-2 fix: year 2026; author was omitted in Pass 1.)_

- [ESTABLISHED] **REGULATORY CONTEXT — France requires disclosure labelling of silhouette-altered advertising images.** Décret n° 2017-738 of 4 May 2017 (implementing Art. 19 of Loi n° 2016-41 of 26 January 2016), in force 1 October 2017: label “Photographie retouchée”; non-compliance fined €37,500 (up to 30% of ad spend). _(Pass-2 missing → added.)_

- [EMERGING] **FRONTIER — GenAI is collapsing previously distinct stages of moodboarding, compositing, set extension, model generation, styling variation and retouching into generative image-editing workflows.** Source: Rizzi & Bertola, “Exploring the generative AI potential in the fashion design process,” _European Journal of Cultural Management and Policy_, Vol. 15, 21 May 2025, DOI `10.3389/ejcmp.2025.13875` (Politecnico di Milano).

- [EMERGING] **FRONTIER — controllable diffusion, rather than unconstrained text-to-image prompting, is becoming the more technically precise research direction for fashion prototyping.** Source: Guopeng Zhang, “Controllable Generative Systems for Fashion Prototyping: A Human–AI Collaborative Framework Based on Diffusion Models,” _Computer Animation and Virtual Worlds_ 37(3), 2026, DOI `10.1002/cav.70155` (Hongik University; single-authored). _(Pass-2 fix: full title; not multi-author.)_

## 2.3 Terminology / open problems / critiques

- [ESTABLISHED] **ADJACENT TERMINOLOGY — durable:** _visual literacy_, _photographic literacy_, _visual hierarchy_, _colour interaction_, _figure–ground_, _art direction_, _fashion photography_, _post-production_, _retouching_, _compositing_. **Frontier:** _synthetic photography_, _generative image editing_, _controllable diffusion_, _reference-conditioned generation_, _identity preservation_, _visual provenance_.

- [ESTABLISHED] **OPEN PROBLEM — representational ethics remains unresolved.** Source: Ozimek, Lainas, Bierhoff & Rohmann, “How photo editing in social media shapes self-perceived attractiveness…,” _BMC Psychology_ 11(1):99, 2023, DOI `10.1186/s40359-023-01143-0`.

- [EMERGING] **OPEN PROBLEM — GenAI introduces a different fidelity problem from conventional retouching: preserving a garment’s exact cut, print, logo, lettering and material cues while modifying model, pose or scene.** Sources: Choi et al., “Improving Diffusion Models for Authentic Virtual Try-on in the Wild” (IDM-VTON), ECCV 2024, arXiv `2403.05139`; Wang et al., FLDM-VTON, IJCAI 2024.

- [ESTABLISHED] **CRITIQUE — “better” fashion imagery cannot safely be operationalised as increasingly perfected bodies.** Retouching disclosure law, self-objectification research and professional retouching ethics make purely aesthetic/engagement metrics methodologically limited.

# 3. 2D animation and motion graphics for fashion narrative

- [ESTABLISHED] **FUNDAMENTAL — fashion moving-image communication has an established research object in the “fashion film.”** Source: Díaz Soloaga & García Guerrero, _Communication & Society_ 29(2), 2016. Priority practice anchor: SHOWstudio / Nick Knight (est. Nov 2000).
