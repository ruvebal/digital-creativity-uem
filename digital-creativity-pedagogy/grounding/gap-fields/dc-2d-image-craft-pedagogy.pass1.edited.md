## 1. Digital drawing pedagogy in fashion and design higher education
State of the art · 2024–2026

[ESTABLISHED] The clearest contemporary pedagogical model is studio-based, assignment-driven learning in which software technique is embedded in visual problems rather than taught only as isolated commands. Curcic's higher-education Digital Drawing study explicitly combines raster freehand drawing with line, volume, value, lighting, texture, colour, perspective and composition, alongside vector techniques; redesigned assignments matched to students' starting drawing ability produced improved outcomes. Dimitrije Curcic, Enhancing Digital Drawing Proficiency: An Examination of Assignment Redesign and Instructional Approaches in Higher Education Contexts (2024), International Journal of Technology in Education and Science 8(4), DOI 10.46328/ijtes.576. 

[EMERGING] A 2025 study specifically examining digital drawing software reports positive relationships between software-supported learning, artistic skill development and students' intentions to continue using digital drawing; its relevance is art education rather than fashion-specific education. Binbin Hu & Ya Li, The role of digital drawing software in enhancing specific artistic skills and behavioral intentions in art education (2025), Cogent Education, DOI 10.1080/2331186X.2025.2481679. 
Taylor & Francis Online

[ESTABLISHED] Fashion pedagogy remains organized around studio critique and iterative design practice, not around a distinctive body of research called "digital drawing pedagogy." A 2025 fashion-pedagogy study explicitly characterizes critique as a signature pedagogy and notes that the fashion-design pedagogy literature itself remains limited. G.S. Yu, With the Heart, the Mind, and the Sight: Cultivating Creativity in Fashion Pedagogy (2025), in Signature Pedagogies for Professional Arts and Design Education. 

[EMERGING] Current fashion-digital-education work is expanding much faster around 3D, VR and GenAI than around raster drawing itself. IFFTI's 2025 strategic-pedagogy work, for example, reports digitized pattern blocks, CLO3D, VR/Gravity Sketch and GenAI experimentation rather than a new 2D-drawing pedagogy. IFFTI, Towards digital transformation of fashion and textiles education: a strategic pedagogy (2025), IFFTI Annual Proceedings, vol. 4. 

[UNVERIFIED] Dedicated research lab blank: I found no verifiable 2024–2026 university laboratory whose central research programme is specifically 2D digital drawing pedagogy for fashion students. The closest recurring communities are IFFTI, DRS/Learn X Design, art-and-design education researchers, and individual fashion-school research teams; the narrow topic does not presently appear institutionalized as a named subfield. The wider scarcity is consistent with Yu's observation that fashion-design pedagogy research remains limited.

[ESTABLISHED] Reflection-in-action remains load-bearing for explaining how drawing/design knowledge develops through cycles of making, seeing consequences and reframing rather than application of rules alone. Donald A. Schön, The Reflective Practitioner: How Professionals Think in Action (1983).

American Academy of Arts and Science

[ESTABLISHED] Studio Thinking remains a canonical arts-education account of studio learning as more than technical mastery, emphasizing habits such as observation, persistence, reflection and judgment. Lois Hetland, Ellen Winner, Shirley Veenema & Kimberly Sheridan, Studio Thinking: The Real Benefits of Visual Arts Education (2007). 
pz.harvard.edu

Open problems / blanks explicitly flagged by the field

[EMERGING] Fashion-AI educators report a broader unresolved problem: how specific technologies should be aligned with learner expertise and actual learning outcomes, rather than simply introduced because they are available. Hyosun An & Minjung Park, AI tools and fashion design education: instructor perspectives on student challenges and design process tool support (2026), Fashion and Textiles 13:3, DOI 10.1186/s40691-025-00452-9.

## 2. Colour management and bitmap literacy for fashion students
State of the art · 2024–2026

[ESTABLISHED] At the technical level, bitmap literacy still requires understanding pixels, channels, bit depth, RGB/CMYK spaces, profiles, device dependence and output conditions. Adobe's current documentation states, for example, that bit depth determines available tonal/colour information and that CMYK working spaces are device-dependent. Adobe, Bit depth and preferences; Color settings in Photoshop [PLATFORM]. 
Adobe Help Centre

[ESTABLISHED] Colour management is technically distinct from colour correction: the former manages transformations between device/profile conditions so appearance can be predicted across capture, display and output. Adobe, Understanding color management [PLATFORM]. 
Adobe Help Centre

[ESTABLISHED] ICC-profile workflows remain the durable infrastructure underlying cross-device colour consistency; Adobe explicitly describes its system as based on International Color Consortium conventions. Adobe, Keeping colors consistent [PLATFORM]. 
Adobe Help Centre

[UNVERIFIED] I found no 2024–2026 empirical fashion-HE study isolating whether students understand bitmap resolution, channels, bit depth, gamut and ICC colour management after instruction. Existing evidence establishes the technical body of knowledge, but not a validated fashion-specific pedagogy for teaching it. 
Adobe Help Centre

Foundational / canonical

[ESTABLISHED] Josef Albers' Interaction of Color remains foundational because it teaches colour relationally through perceptual exercises rather than as fixed swatches or numerical recipes; originally published by Yale University Press in 1963, it remains in print as an instructional text. 
Yale Books

[ESTABLISHED] Digital-colour science distinguishes perceptual colour, device characterization, colour representation and reproduction; Sharma & Trussell's survey remains a useful canonical technical overview. Gaurav Sharma & H. Joel Trussell, Digital Color Imaging (2001).

[UNVERIFIED] No validated threshold or assessment instrument was found for deciding when a fashion student possesses sufficient bitmap literacy rather than merely knowing application commands.

Adjacent / sharper terminology

[ESTABLISHED] Colour management — durable technical term covering device profiles, working spaces, conversion and rendering. 
Adobe Help Centre

[ESTABLISHED] Digital colour imaging — durable research terminology encompassing colour representation, capture, processing, display and reproduction. 

[ESTABLISHED] Raster graphics / raster imaging is technically sharper than "bitmap" when discussing normal photographic or painted images; bit depth, pixels and channels are properties of this representation. Adobe [PLATFORM]. 
Adobe Help Centre

[ESTABLISHED] Gamut mapping, colour-space conversion, ICC profiling, soft proofing are the more precise terms when the intended learning concerns predictable output rather than general colour theory. Adobe [PLATFORM].

Adobe Help Centre

Current fashion-education research describes design curricula as moving through inspiration, conceptualization, exploration, refinement and communication. An & Park (2026). 

[EMERGING] Rizzi et al.'s 2025 Artificial A(i)rchive laboratory at Politecnico di Milano is a particularly relevant named teaching/research site: master's students used image- and video-generative systems during fashion ideation and development, with researchers examining their benefits and shortcomings.
