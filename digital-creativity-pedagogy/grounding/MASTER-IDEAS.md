# Master ideas for CD I–II

Instructor-side trend map for the 15 official Creación Digital lessons. This is a
forge aid, not a second syllabus and not student-facing bibliography. It answers a
practical question for each unit: **what durable idea must survive tool changes, and
what emerging field movement should the lesson name without chasing novelty?**

## Reading the labels

- **Established** = durable field knowledge, a repeated research object, or a
  resolved Ahmes node that can safely support the narrower claim stated here.
- **Emerging** = a visible 2024–2026 direction, current practice, or early research
  signal. It is a prompt for critical comparison, not a settled learning-outcome
  claim.
- **Boundary** = the claim that must not be smuggled into the lesson. A vector hit is
  never used as a citation; unsafe or bibliography-only hits remain visible as gaps.

The map is deliberately tool-independent. A tool can demonstrate the idea, but the
idea is what should remain assessable when the interface, model, or platform changes.

## The sequence at a glance

`image as system → drawing as thinking → colour as code → effects as transformation → form as spatial literacy → volume as behaviour → references as situated reuse → motion as time → still life as material attention → retouch as ethical post-production → avatar as identity system → experience as audience relation → video as fashion-film narrative → portfolio as evidence → AR as interaction choice`

## Lesson-by-lesson master ideas

### I.1 · Introducción a las imágenes digitales

**Master idea — an image is a cultural and communicative system, not merely a file.**
Students should be able to explain what an image makes visible, for whom, through
which conventions, and with what omissions.

- **Established:** fashion-image literacy joins visual hierarchy, framing, body,
  garment, styling, context, and circulation. The durable reference is Shinkle’s
  account of fashion photography as constructed looking, not neutral capture
  `(Shinkle 2008, 15)`, `evaluator_safe=yes` in the existing matrix.
- **Emerging:** fashion communication is increasingly transmedia and algorithmically
  mediated; image posts, short video, shows, web, and immersive media participate in
  one distributed brand system. A FACTUM hit surfaced the adjacent curriculum need
  for information validation, effective prompting, and critical thinking, but its
  node is currently `[BIBLIO-GAP]` (confidence 0.70, metadata-derived author).
- **Nail in the lesson:** every introductory image task needs a one-sentence
  audience/purpose statement and a provenance note, even when no AI is used.

### I.2 · Tecnología digital 2D: Herramientas de dibujo

**Master idea — drawing is externalised visual thinking, not button fluency.**
Digital drawing should make alternatives, decisions, and spatial or material
hypotheses visible before the student commits to a polished image.

- **Established:** sketching, mark, proportion, visual hierarchy, iteration, and
  reference-to-variation remain tool-independent foundations. The lesson should
  assess a sequence of decisions, not the number of functions demonstrated.
- **Emerging:** fashion ideation is moving toward human–AI collaborative sketching,
  image variation, and controllable generation. The Profield search surfaced
  *Generative Artificial Intelligence in Collaborative Ideation*, but the relevant
  node is `[BIBLIO-GAP]`; retain the direction as a trend signal, not an ordinary
  citation.
- **Nail in the lesson:** require three visibly different alternatives and a short
  selection rationale; AI-generated variants, if used, are inputs to judgement, not
  substitutes for drawing decisions.

### I.3 · Color, imágenes con mapas de bits

**Master idea — colour is relational, cultural, and time-sensitive, not a palette
chosen once.** Students should connect colour interaction to contrast, material,
light, skin/garment context, and intended response.

- **Established:** colour interaction and perceptual organisation are durable
  knowledge; the tool is only the current instrument for testing relationships.
- **Emerging:** platform-native editing styles make colour trends circulate quickly.
  Roivainen’s resolved node documents how creator technologies and popular editing
  styles produce time-bound lighting, colour, and tonal conventions `(Roivainen
  2025, 8)`, `evaluator_safe=yes`.
- **Nail in the lesson:** students submit a colour comparison, not a single “final”
  palette: what changed, what remained legible, and which cultural or platform code
  they are invoking.

### I.4 · Efectos

**Master idea — an effect is a semantic transformation, not decoration.** Every
  blur, composite, generative extension, distortion, or atmosphere changes what the
  viewer believes about body, garment, space, or evidence.

- **Established:** effects belong to art direction and post-production reasoning:
  intent → transformation → check. “Better” is not equivalent to more spectacular,
  more perfect, or more synthetic.
- **Emerging:** generative image editing and controllable diffusion are collapsing
  moodboarding, compositing, set extension, styling variation, and retouching into
  one workflow. Rizzi’s resolved fashion-design study supports treating AI as a
  process collaborator, not a replacement `(Rizzi 2025, 10)`, `evaluator_safe=yes`.
- **Nail in the lesson:** every effect must have an intended meaning and a reversible
  process trace; students must identify what factual or representational information
  the effect may have altered.

### I.5 · Fundamentos y representación de la forma tridimensional

**Master idea — 3D software is a spatial-literacy instrument before it is a modelling
  interface.** The student must understand form, view, proportion, and relation
  between 2D evidence and 3D interpretation.

- **Established:** spatial ability is a distinct learning concern in apparel design.
  Moritz’s resolved node connects spatial ability with design visualisation and CLO
  3D `(Moritz 2022, 16)`, `evaluator_safe=yes`.
- **Emerging:** immersive sketching, virtual prototyping, and AI-assisted 3D
  authoring are lowering the threshold for exploring form. Do not transfer generic
  mesh/CSG pedagogy into fashion without a garment/form boundary.
- **Nail in the lesson:** assess a 2D-to-3D translation diagnostic and a view-change
  explanation; do not grade only software operation or render polish.

### I.6 · Volumen

**Master idea — volume is behaviour: fit, drape, material response, and movement,
not a mesh that merely looks three-dimensional.**

- **Established:** digital garment learning joins pattern, fit correction, drape,
  material properties, avatar relation, and iterative comparison. The resolved
  Galada node names 3D garment simulation, fit correction, pattern making, spatial
  visualisation, and skill testing as one educational field `(Galada 2025, 1)`,
  `evaluator_safe=yes`; Coats 2026 remains the course-structure anchor in the
  matrix.
- **Emerging:** the field is moving toward multi-view, motion-aware, and size-aware
  garment representations, but these are technical frontiers rather than evidence
  that one classroom sequence works generally.
- **Nail in the lesson:** require one fit/material diagnosis across at least two
  views or states, with a verbal explanation of what the simulation reveals and what
  it cannot reveal.

### I.7 · Fuentes y referencias compositivas en el diseño de moda

**Master idea — reference is a situated act of selection and recontextualisation,
not image collecting.** A reference board should make an argument, not just display
 taste.

- **Established:** moodboards train image thinking by connecting distant elements,
  concepts, stories, emotions, and arguments. The relevant collaborative-ideation
  node is currently `[BIBLIO-GAP]`, so this remains a bounded field signal rather
  than a clean lesson citation.
- **Emerging:** AI retrieval and generation increase the speed and scale of visual
  reference production while raising provenance, attribution, cultural-appropriation,
  and stylistic-convergence questions.
- **Nail in the lesson:** every board needs source, reason for selection, cultural
  position, transformation, and a statement of what the student deliberately did not
  copy.

### I.8 · Creación de imágenes digitales animadas

**Master idea — motion communicates through time.** Timing, rhythm, transition,
  anticipation, staging, and sequence are meaning-making decisions, not a novelty
  layer added after a still image.

- **Established:** animation principles and fashion-film grammar provide durable
  temporal knowledge. Díaz Soloaga and García Guerrero’s resolved node defines the
  fashion film as branded audiovisual communication `(Díaz Soloaga & García Guerrero
  2016, 5)`, `evaluator_safe=yes`.
- **Emerging:** image-to-video, generative video, hybrid 2D/3D, and motion-conditioned
  fashion media are expanding quickly. Rizzi’s resolved node confirms image/video
  generation as part of the contemporary fashion-design tool landscape `(Rizzi 2025,
  5)`, `evaluator_safe=yes`; it does not validate an animation pedagogy.
- **Nail in the lesson:** students submit a timing/storyboard rationale and compare
  motion choices against a still version; novelty of movement cannot substitute for
  narrative or visual clarity.

### I.9 · Bodegones digitales

**Master idea — still life is material attention under controlled conditions.** The
lesson should make students reason about object relation, surface, light, scale,
shadow, and the difference between depicting a product and constructing its meaning.

- **Established:** composition, lighting, materiality, figure/ground, and controlled
  observation are durable image-making knowledge; the corpus does not provide a
  resolved fashion-specific still-life pedagogy source.
- **Emerging:** digital product visualisation, virtual sampling, and synthetic product
  imagery are moving the still life toward a hybrid of studio photography, 3D render,
  and branded product world. This remains a field trend, not a measured teaching
  outcome in the present corpus.
- **Nail in the lesson:** require two materially different treatments of the same
  object and a note explaining what each treatment makes desirable, credible, or
  ambiguous.

### II.1 · Retoque fotográfico avanzado de imágenes moda

**Master idea — retouching is ethical post-production and visual authorship, not
  perfection labour.** Students need to know what was changed, why, and with what
  representational consequence.

- **Established:** photographic editing is skilled situated practice; Roivainen’s
  resolved study shows editing styles are learned, shared, and historically variable
  `(Roivainen 2025, 8)`, `evaluator_safe=yes`. The existing Ozimek-style coat remains
  `[BIBLIO-GAP]` for the narrower appearance-psychology claim.
- **Emerging:** synthetic photography, generative fill, identity preservation, and
  garment-fidelity problems are replacing the old clean split between capture and
  retouch. Rizzi’s human–AI process anchor is relevant by transfer, not a retouching
  curriculum validation.
- **Nail in the lesson:** pair every before/after with an edit ledger and a body/
  garment/scene-impact statement; do not let “professional” mean “more idealised.”

### II.2 · Creación de avatares

**Master idea — an avatar is an identity-and-body system, not a neutral mannequin.**
Students must justify viewpoint, defaults, body representation, styling, and
authorship.

- **Established:** Gonzalez-Franco et al. found that first-person versus third-person
  avatar customisation affected embodiment of a larger avatar; the resolved node is a
  bounded perspective/ethics anchor `(Gonzalez-Franco et al. 2024, 12)`,
  `evaluator_safe=yes`.
- **Emerging:** digital humans, avatar identity play, inferred/biometric data, and
  non-photorealistic or non-human bodies are expanding the design space. These are
  identity and privacy prompts, not proof of an avatar-making teaching sequence.
- **Nail in the lesson:** require an identity-intent note, a default audit, and a
  human decision checkpoint; VTON remains out of scope.

### II.3 · Diseño de experiencias de moda digitales

**Master idea — design the audience’s experience, not just the digital object.**
Presence, navigation, observation, reflection, embodiment, and access are part of
the fashion artefact.

- **Established:** Kim’s resolved VR-fashion study supports a distinction between
  screened and immersive stimulus types `(Kim 2023, 6)`, `evaluator_safe=yes`;
  Smith-Glaviana supports observation, reflection, multiple views, and close-ups in
  a digital fashion exhibition `(Smith-Glaviana 2025, 1)`, `evaluator_safe=yes`.
- **Emerging:** digital exhibitions are moving toward mixed reality, virtual worlds,
  interactive objects, and platform-mediated participation. Experience criteria must
  remain distinct from claims that a particular platform or teaching method works.
- **Nail in the lesson:** every experience brief names the intended visitor action,
  evidence of engagement, reflective prompt, and access fallback.

### II.4 · Recursos digitales de edición de vídeo

**Master idea — fashion video is a branded narrative system before it is a timeline
of cuts.** Purpose, audience, platform, consent, sound, captions, and pacing belong
inside the edit.

- **Established:** Díaz Soloaga and García Guerrero frame fashion film as a distinct
  audiovisual/branded-content format `(Díaz Soloaga & García Guerrero 2016, 5)`,
  `evaluator_safe=yes`.
- **Emerging:** short-form platform circulation, generative image/video, virtual
  fashion films, and motion-rich digital brand worlds are expanding the field. Rizzi
  supports the presence of image/video generation in fashion design `(Rizzi 2025,
  5)`, but not a video-editing pedagogy.
- **Nail in the lesson:** grade the production process—brief, storyboard, capture,
  edit, caption/consent pass, export—not only the final spectacle.

### II.5 · Maquetación, edición y creación de web y portfolio digital

**Master idea — a portfolio is evidence of learning, authorship, and professional
identity, not a gallery grid or a promise of employment.**

- **Established:** Garcia’s resolved study treats self-coded portfolios as a
  project-based technical assessment and evidence of front-end/design competence
  `(Garcia 2025, 2, 4)`, `evaluator_safe=yes`; Anwar and Rizzi support visible
  authorship and human–AI process in studio education.
- **Emerging:** multimodal portfolios, platform-native identity, AI provenance,
  responsive/accessibility expectations, and algorithmic visibility are converging.
  Fashion-specific causal evidence linking UX to hiring remains an explicit gap.
- **Nail in the lesson:** require brief, process trail, tool/material declaration,
  accessibility checks, credits, and a reflection on what the page makes visible or
  hides; do not teach a trendy stack as a hiring law.

### II.6 · Hologramas y R.A.

**Master idea — AR is an interaction and audience choice, not a technology demo.**
The student must explain why the experience needs AR, what the audience does, and
what the medium costs.

- **Established:** Sarkis et al.’s resolved fashion-AR study connects AR with purchase
  decisions, customer engagement, and brand loyalty in an 855-person Middle Eastern
  consumer sample `(Sarkis et al. 2026, 2, 8)`, `evaluator_safe=yes`. This is an
  audience/industry phenomenon, not pedagogy.
- **Emerging:** AR try-on, spatial retail, immersive brand experience, and mixed
  reality continue to expand; “holographic fashion” remains a thin and unstable
  scholarly label rather than a settled subfield.
- **Nail in the lesson:** require a medium-choice rationale, audience interaction
  storyboard, access/cost note, and a non-AR fallback; keep the unit as a declared
  pilot and keep VTON out of scope.

## Cross-cutting ideas to repeat across the course

1. **Process beats output:** brief → reference → iteration → decision → export.
2. **Human judgement remains assessable:** students explain selection, transformation,
   rejection, and verification.
3. **Representation is never neutral:** body, identity, material, culture, and
   platform defaults need naming.
4. **Audience is part of the artefact:** every medium choice carries an intended
   action, access condition, and response.
5. **Emerging technology is a design question:** use it where it changes the
   problem—not as proof of currency.
