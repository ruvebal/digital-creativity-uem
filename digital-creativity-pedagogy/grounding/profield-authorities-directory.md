## 7. OPEN PROBLEMS / BLANKS (audit-refined)

**Confirmed unresolved technical/scholarly blanks:** garment fidelity, fit, multi-view consistency, scarce/biased fashion datasets, authorship/process visibility in GenAI education, and robust evaluation of immersive-fashion UX. Recent VTON work continues to frame garment detail, size/fit and arbitrary-view consistency as open problems (IDM-VTON ECCV 2024; VTON 360 CVPR 2025; 2026 fit-benchmark work https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08526).

**`disputed:` “2D motion graphics for fashion” as an unresolved technical blank.** Shortage is primarily a **bibliographic/subfield-label problem**, not evidence that the underlying design questions lack answers: fashion-film, audiovisual-design, VFX, post-production and motion-design scholarship already cover much of the territory (Jódar Marín explicitly studies image processing, VFX and infographics in fashion films).

**Confirmed appropriate blank:** “holographic fashion” is poorly established as a distinct scholarly subfield vs AR/VR/XR, VTON and immersive fashion; keeping it as a search blank is methodologically appropriate rather than filling from event-marketing claims.

**`missing:` VTON section** should include newer 2025–2026 fit- and view-aware work (VTON 360; 2026 fit-benchmark papers), not only IDM-VTON / CatVTON / StableVITON.

**`missing:` fashion-GenAI education map** understates 2026 institutional infrastructure (LCF International Symposium on AI in Fashion / FAIREE proceedings).

**`missing:` colour/digital-material pipeline** — UAL VP/XR has produced colour-workflow and CG-fabric reproduction work with virtual-production technologies.
