## Methods

### `figurin-digital` — Digital figurín (fashion croquis figure)

- **family/mode:** drawing · constructive
- **source:** held · `gap`
- **Sandra:** ACT1
- **units:** I.1, I.2
- **time / group:** 45 min · individual
- **evidence:** gap: fashion-HE figurín sequence not page-verified in Wave-1 references
- **steps:**
  1. Set a visible proportion scaffold (head-count or grid) on a blank canvas.
  1. Block the figure as simple volumes before any garment detail.
  1. Add garment silhouette with construction lines distinct from finish lines.
  1. Export a process pair: scaffold-only + finished figurín.
  1. Note one proportion decision that would survive a medium change (hand → raster → vector).

### `photobash-integration` — Photobash integration (key-visual collage craft)

- **family/mode:** bitmap · synthetic
- **source:** gap · `gap`
- **Sandra:** ACT2
- **units:** I.3, I.4
- **time / group:** 60 min · pairs
- **evidence:** untested — classroom craft aligned to Sandra ACT2 key visual
- **steps:**
  1. Collect a short, attributed contact sheet (≤12 stills) for one key-visual brief.
  1. Cut and layer fragments so seams are intentional, not accidental.
  1. Unify light/colour with adjustment layers only (keep originals recoverable).
  1. Check Gestalt grouping: what reads as one figure vs background noise?
  1. Export flat + layered file; list three integration decisions in a process note.

### `gestalt-composition` — Gestalt composition pass (grouping / figure–ground)

- **family/mode:** composition · constructive
- **source:** gap · `gap`
- **Sandra:** ACT2
- **units:** I.3, I.4, I.7, I.9
- **time / group:** 25 min · pairs
- **evidence:** gap: Arnheim / Gestalt pedagogy not Wave-1 verified
- **steps:**
  1. Name one Gestalt relation to test (proximity, similarity, closure, continuity, or figure–ground).
  1. Apply the relation once to a key visual or bodegón draft.
  1. Invert or break the relation in a second version; keep both.
  1. Peer names which version groups more clearly and why (one sentence).
  1. Record the chosen relation in the process note for portfolio.
