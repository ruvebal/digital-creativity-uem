# DC unit enrichment packs

These packs are the pre-forge research layer for lessons and student decks,
following the stronger TC pattern: official contract → scoped discovery →
citation verification → critical field lens → slideshow spine.

Athanor/vector results are discovery evidence only. A record becomes a
student-facing claim only after Ahmes resolves its node/page citation. Until
then the pack records the canonical DOI and `[BIBLIO-GAP]`. Ibero-American and
Spain routes are critical counterweights, not decorative regional examples.
