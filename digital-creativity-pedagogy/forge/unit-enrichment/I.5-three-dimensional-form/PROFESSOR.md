# I.5 · Fundamentals and Representation of Three-Dimensional Form — enrichment

## Forge thesis

Three-dimensional form is spatial literacy, not a feature revealed by a 3D
application. A student first makes a two-dimensional reading inspectable, then
tests it through axis, silhouette, plane, view, and a deliberately simple
volume.

## Strong conceptual routes

- **Sequence before interface:** Papahristou and Zolota Tatsi (2024, 5) report
  a taught fashion-programme sequence in which 3D virtual prototyping follows
  earlier 2D patternmaking knowledge and is divided between theory and a
  practical/laboratory half. It supports teaching a sequence, not claiming a
  validated outcome for this classroom.
- **A second curricular case:** the same review reports progressive 3D
  integration at AMFI (Papahristou and Zolota Tatsi 2024, 3): from a final
  prototyping phase into the conceptual phase. Treat this as a comparison, not
  a prescription.
- **Observable spatial reasoning:** ask what a front, side, and top view must
  agree on before a blockout is accepted. A plausible render is not, by itself,
  evidence that the student understood the volume.
- **Low-tech first:** paper, pencil, and primitive solids are legitimate
  diagnostic media; the individual problem remains solvable without AI or a
  modelling application.

## Critical / inclusion lens

Spatial conventions and default figures can make some bodies, materials, and
forms easier to represent than others. Ask which dimensions, view, or
silhouette the representation treats as normal, and what becomes invisible as
a result. This is an open studio question, not a claim about the cohort.

## Open living prompt

**What does this view make legible, and which form does it quietly treat as the
standard?**

## Slideshow spine — media acceptance pending

1. A 3D model starts with a reading of a 2D trace.
2. Axis, proportion, silhouette, plane, and view must agree.
3. A render is not proof of spatial understanding.
4. 2D before 3D is a documented course structure, not a universal rule.
5. Lab: make the sketch → primitive volume → digital representation trace.
6. Carry forward the view that your model cannot yet explain.

The public student deck is blocked until an accepted MP5 collection supplies
the cover, analysis, masterclass, and Lab media slots. Do not substitute a
tool-logo, a vendor render, or generated imagery.
