# NM campaign allocation index

**Rule:** one campaign or campaign-action case is allocated to exactly one NM
lesson across the course. Its name, alias, canonical source, and teaching use
may repeat inside that lesson; they may not recur as a case in another lesson,
deck, lab, or assignment. Repeated scholarly concepts and sources are not
campaign cases.

Before forging or revising a unit, check the Profield pointer in
`~/src/profield/runs/fashion-exemplary-social-campaigns/CANONICAL.md`, then
choose a ranked, URL-verified case that fits the unit and is not allocated
below. If the ranked bank lacks a critical/adverse advertising case, add a
clearly labelled supplemental case supported by primary documentation and/or
reliable consumer/regulatory research; do not assign it a Profield rank. Update
this index and the lesson's `campaign_ids` front matter together. Treat aliases
and hashtags as the same case. The validator checks all forged NM lesson front
matter and searches lesson and deck copy for cross-unit reuse.

The allocation favours course-wide variety while preserving a fit with each
unit's focus. Profield rank is the case's teaching priority, not a measure of
campaign quality or social impact.
