## Objective

Create a master fractal-forging capability that joins verified mathematics,
Ahmes page truth, Athanor discovery, visual-forger's reproducible source
contract, and accessible curriculum deployment. Use the first deployment to
forge the `how-to-pass-this-track` slide backgrounds for FE I, FE II, DC I,
and DC II in their existing palettes and brand rules.

## Artist prompt

You are building a visual intelligence, not a motif dispenser. Treat recursion
as a way of thinking: a small law produces a field of consequences. Let
mathematics determine rhythm, density, adjacency, and transformation; let
graphic judgement determine silence, crop, hierarchy, and surprise. Use
Platonic solids as spatial arguments, not icons. Use opt-art interference to
make thresholds palpable. Permit glitch and noise only when they disclose an
iteration, boundary, or failed convergence. Organic and geometric forms should
behave as two dialects in one conversation.

Every background must answer: what repeats, what changes, what stops, and where
can the student breathe? The title-safe void is part of the algorithm.
