## Context & learning goals

Teach retouching as a chain of accountable choices. Students will separate
technical repair from representational change, use a non-destructive workflow,
record edits, and explain how a change affects consent, body representation, or
material truth.

## Session A: Repair, transform, disclose (2 hours)

| Time | Teacher move |
| --- | --- |
| 00–10 | Show a neutral repair and a representational alteration; ask where the ethical boundary moves. |
| 10–30 | Explain masks, healing, colour, reshape, liquify, layer history, disclosure, and approval. |
| 30–45 | Screen a rights-cleared retouch breakdown; students label repair, enhancement, and transformation. |
| 45–55 | Compare edit-ledgers from two images; discuss what a viewer would need to know. |
| 55–65 | Demo a non-destructive stack with before/after toggles and an edit ledger. |
| 65–100 | Studio sprint: make a repair pass and a clearly disclosed creative pass. |
| 100–113 | Individual B3, no AI: diagnostic disclosure, undisclosed-distortion identification, two-sentence disclosure. |
| 113–120 | Exit ticket: “The image remains accountable when ___ is recorded.” |

## Screening & analysis

- **Video:** process breakdown with permission; pause before each intervention.
- **Stills:** repair exemplar, undisclosed alteration counterexample, and a
  material/detail close-up; no real person’s image without permission.
- **Graphic:** `source → edit → representational effect → disclosure → approval`
  plus a plain-language edit-ledger template.

## Studio sprint

Submit source, repair version, creative version, layer history, edit ledger,
and a short disclosure. Use supplied or self-created model imagery with a
documented permission route; do not use classmates as unconsented subjects.

## Individual problem

1. **Diagnostic.** Given a retouched image with a visibly altered body proportion and no disclosure line, name exactly what is missing from an honest disclosure and write it.
2. Given a piece description — *"the editor smoothed skin texture, narrowed the waist by an unstated amount, and added no credit or disclosure"* — identify which specific change makes this cross from craft into undisclosed distortion.
3. **No-AI, declared:** without AI assistance, write a two-sentence disclosure for a described edit (background replacement + minor skin retouching, garment untouched).

## Critical field lens & open living prompt

**Field tension:** retouching distributes authority over bodies, garments,
truth claims, and professional appearance.

**Critical perspective:** Who has the right to decide that an image needs
correction, and what must remain visible after the edit?

**Open living prompt:** When does technical repair become a social demand to
look different, and what evidence would justify refusing the change?

**Bring forward:** one edit-ledger entry, subject/garment impact, or refusal case.
