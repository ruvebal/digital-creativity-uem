Teach solids as **readable structure**, not mesh polish — same honesty as I.6 fit signs.

| --- | --- | --- |
| Tetrahedron | equilateral triangles | smallest closed volume; tension / strut metaphor |
| Cube | squares | enclosure, stack, Mondrian/Munari square lineage |
