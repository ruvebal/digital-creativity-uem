- **S1** Metropolitan Museum of Art. “Dress,” Raymond Duncan, 1920s. https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/87582 — ancient peplos description and modern fabric/pattern distinctions, object record. Not evidence of a direct chain to modern fashion.
- **S2** Aragonès Riu, Núria. 2016. “Isadora Duncan and Fashion: Classical Revival and Modernity.” *Datatèxtil* 34: 31–38. https://diposit.ub.edu/items/20d0ab01-cda1-47c6-b06c-3e7ac4a592af — repository record: Duncan/classical revival; full article and precise pages still need Ahmes verification.
- **S3** Getty Research Institute. “Experimental Theater.” https://www.getty.edu/research/exhibitions_events/exhibitions/bauhaus/new_artist/body_spirit/theater/ — 1922 Stuttgart premiere, costume transformation and development independent of Bauhaus. Restriction is a comparative hypothesis needing performance/costume evidence, not asserted by this record.
- **S4** Museum at FIT. “Dance & Fashion,” 2014. https://exhibitions.fitnyc.edu/dance-and-fashion/ — Graham's *Lamentation* jersey tube and movement; exhibition interpretation. Exhibited object is a 21st-century costume, not an original performance photograph.
- **S5** Pruska-Oldenhof, Izabella. 2016. “Loïe Fuller's Serpentines and Poetics of Self-Abnegation in the Era of Electrotechnics.” https://doi.org/10.1093/oxfordhb/9780199981601.013.2 — scholarly lead; full text/page entailment not checked. No specific claim about Fuller is yet release-ready.
- **S6** LIFE. “Fred Astaire: The Dance Legend Is About To Be Discovered All Over Again.” https://www.life.com/arts-entertainment/fred-astaire-the-dance-legend-is-about-to-be-discovered-all-over-again/ — identifies Astaire/Rogers in *Carefree* (1938), credit Rex Hardy/Life Picture Collection/Shutterstock, and Astaire/Hayworth in *You Never Were Lovelier* (1942), John Florea/Life Picture Collection/Shutterstock. Gallery is a primary media object, not film scholarship or image clearance.
- **S7** *The Oxford Handbook of Screendance Studies*. https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/34652 — research lead for screen dance, full source and specific film-analysis pages pending. The framing/editing proposition in the spine is an inquiry, not a verified historical conclusion.
- **S8** Aschieri, Patricia. 2013. *Subjetividad en movimiento: reapropiaciones de la danza butoh en Argentina*. Universidad de Buenos Aires. https://repositorio.filo.uba.ar/entities/publication/a72ad93a-54d2-493c-8ec0-def0e95c9133 — repository abstract describes Argentine arrival and cultural reworking; specific pages and distinct performers' accounts pending. The thesis's CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 is not a license for its embedded images.
- **S9** Desmond, Jane C. 1991. “Dancing Out the Difference: Cultural Imperialism and Ruth St. Denis's ‘Radha’ of 1906.” *Signs* 17 (1): 28–49. https://doi.org/10.1086/494712 — research lead on exoticized staging; full text and claim scope pending.
- **S10** Tyler, Imogen. 2009. “Against Abjection.” *Feminist Theory* 10 (1): 77–98. https://eprints.lancs.ac.uk/id/eprint/27150/ — abstract explicitly warns about repeating disgust toward maternal bodies and calls for social/political analysis; applying it to particular designers is unverified interpretation.
- **S11** Villena Alarcón, Eduardo. 2018. “El tratamiento informativo de la moda en la prensa española: el caso de David Delfín.” https://idus.us.es/items/043b387a-6b77-4619-9d6f-9f3e862f817e — record for press-reception research; precise collection and reported findings need full text/page check.
- **S12** Ana Locking. “About.” https://analocking.com/about/ — artist's situated self-description of body, identity and fashion; not independent proof of effects or abjection.
- **S13** Fédération de la Haute Couture et de la Mode. “Matières Fécales.” https://www.fhcm.paris/en/house/matieres-fecales — organization describes 2025 Paris label and earlier practice; its promotional account is not independent critique and grants no image rights.
- **S14** Baird, Bruce. 2022. “Prologue and Introduction.” In *A History of Butô*, 1–19. https://doi.org/10.1093/oso/9780197630273.003.0001 — publisher abstract situates Hijikata, divergent styles and worldwide expansion; full chapter and pages pending.
- **S15** Candelario, Rosemary. 2016. “Butoh.” *Routledge Encyclopedia of Modernism*. https://www.rem.routledge.com/articles/butoh-1 — research account of different approaches and diversity; full article/page and resolver pending.
