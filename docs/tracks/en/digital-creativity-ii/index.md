## How to complete a unit

Each lesson gives you **B1** (concept and debate), **B2** (studio deliverable and definition of done), and **B3** (individual problem-solving). Submit a piece ID, process folder, final artefact, process note, file-format/accessibility checks, and AI/material declaration. For II.2, II.3, II.5 and II.6, the public-showroom checklists are unsigned drafts only: no public exhibition or research reuse is authorized without the separate consent and DPO gates. Dates and institutional assessment procedures remain in the Campus Virtual.
