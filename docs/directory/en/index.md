<h2>Prizes</h2>
<div class="field-card-grid">
<article class="field-card" id="prizes--lvmh-prize">
<h3 class="field-card__title">LVMH Prize for Young Fashion Designers</h3>
<p class="field-card__summary">Major industry prize for emerging designers (incl. Karl Lagerfeld and Savoir-Faire prizes). Useful as a career-field landmark, not as a studio grading rubric.</p>
<p class="field-card__badges"><span class="field-card__status">established</span><span class="field-card__meta">international</span><span class="field-card__meta">ongoing</span></p>
<p class="field-card__link"><a href="https://www.lvmhprize.com/" rel="noopener noreferrer">www.lvmhprize.com/</a></p>
</article>
<article class="field-card" id="prizes--andam-prize">
<h3 class="field-card__title">ANDAM Prize</h3>
<p class="field-card__summary">Canonical French emerging-designer prize; past honorees include Margiela, Vaccarello, Van Herpen — a map pin for “where designers become visible.”</p>
<p class="field-card__badges"><span class="field-card__status">established</span><span class="field-card__meta">France</span><span class="field-card__meta">ongoing</span></p>
<p class="field-card__link"><a href="https://andam.fr/" rel="noopener noreferrer">andam.fr/</a></p>
</article>
<article class="field-card" id="prizes--woolmark-prize">
<h3 class="field-card__title">International Woolmark Prize</h3>
<p class="field-card__summary">Emerging-designer prize with material constraint (wool); another consecration path beside LVMH/ANDAM.</p>
<p class="field-card__badges"><span class="field-card__status">established</span><span class="field-card__meta">international</span><span class="field-card__meta">ongoing</span></p>
<p class="field-card__link"><a href="https://www.woolmarkprize.com/" rel="noopener noreferrer">www.woolmarkprize.com/</a></p>
</article>
<article class="field-card" id="prizes--global-change-award">
<h3 class="field-card__title">Global Change Award (H&M Foundation)</h3>
<p class="field-card__summary">Sustainability/innovation award often cited in fashion-innovation discourse — industry framing more than peer-reviewed pedagogy.</p>
<p class="field-card__badges"><span class="field-card__status">practitioner</span><span class="field-card__meta">international</span><span class="field-card__meta">ongoing</span></p>
<p class="field-card__link"><a href="https://globalchangeaward.com/" rel="noopener noreferrer">globalchangeaward.com/</a></p>
