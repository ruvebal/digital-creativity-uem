# D2 · Transposition

**Digital Creativity I · research and projects · 10% of the course grade**

> **Submit before 23 November 2026 through Campus Virtual.** Campus Virtual is the binding source for the exact submission time and file channel.

## The assignment in one sentence

Make a **cartel / poster image** from a work in another language: a song, film, poem, literary work, theatre piece, or comparable source. An image series is welcome when it works as one coherent visual proposal.

D2 is the same bilingual brief as **ACT3 Transposición**. It is one deliverable, not a second task for another cohort.

## What “transposition” means here

Do not illustrate the source or decorate it with borrowed imagery. Identify what the source does — its tension, rhythm, point of view, atmosphere, conflict, or question — then make visual decisions that give it a new form.

Your image must be able to stand on its own while making the source relationship legible. Composition, colour, hierarchy, typography, material treatment, and scale are part of the argument.

## What to submit

Upload the final work and a compact process package through Campus Virtual:

- **Final cartel / poster image**, or a coherent image series, exported clearly enough to inspect.
- **Editable working file or accessible working link**, when the chosen tool permits it.
- **Source declaration:** title, author/maker, date where known, format, and a usable source/credit record.
- **Short transposition statement:** what you carried across from the source, what you changed, and why.
- **Visual research and process trail:** references, sketches, tests, alternatives, and at least one revision or rejected route.
- **Authorship, material, and AI declaration** for this piece. Follow the [AI usage declaration]({{ '/ai-declaration/' | relative_url }}): record **Tools used**, **Purpose of use**, **Human contribution**, and **Source material**. “No AI tools used” is a valid declaration.

Credit all source material and name collaborators where applicable. A source credit is not permission to copy: transform, test, and make your own visual argument.

## Internal rubric

This rubric gives the **D2 task score out of 10**. The criterion shares below add up to the task score; they are not additional course percentages. D2 contributes **10%** to the subject grade as part of the official **Research and projects (20%)** category.

| Criterion | Share | 9–10 · Excellent | 7–8.9 · Competent | 5–6.9 · Minimum pass | 0–4.9 · Insufficient |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Transposition and critical idea** | 30% | A precise, surprising visual argument transforms the source rather than illustrating it. | The source is reworked through a clear visual idea. | The source is identifiable but the transformation is literal or thin. | No legible transformation, or copied/undeclared material replaces an idea. |
| **Visual communication** | 25% | Composition, hierarchy, colour, typography, and material choices make the intended reading compelling. | Visual choices are coherent and readable. | Core message is readable but choices are generic, uneven, or unresolved. | Communication is confusing, illegible, or unconsidered. |
| **Source relation and credits** | 20% | Source, references, permissions/limits, and visual lineage are exact and critically handled. | Source and key references are credited; relation is explained. | Basic source credit is present but relation or record is incomplete. | Source is missing, misleading, or uncredited. |
| **Process, revision, and authorship** | 15% | Process shows purposeful alternatives, feedback or self-critique, revision, and a complete declaration. | Clear process trail and declaration; at least one meaningful revision. | Some process evidence, but little testing, revision, or incomplete declaration. | No credible process trail, no declaration, or authorship cannot be explained. |
| **Technical delivery and accessibility** | 10% | File is reliable, well prepared, readable, and suitable for viewing/sharing. | Submission is accessible and technically sound. | Minor technical, export, or readability problems. | Unusable, missing, or technically unreadable submission. |

## Submission checklist

- [ ] Final image or coherent series is ready to view.
- [ ] Source and references are credited.

- [ ] Process includes tests and at least one change of direction.
- [ ] Authorship, material, and AI declaration is complete.
- [ ] The submitted file/link opens and remains readable.
- [ ] Uploaded to Campus Virtual **before 23 November 2026**.
