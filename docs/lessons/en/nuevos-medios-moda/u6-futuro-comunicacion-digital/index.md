## Teaching language

**This course is taught in Spanish.** Use the [Spanish lesson]({{ '/lessons/es/nuevos-medios-moda/u6-futuro-comunicacion-digital/' | relative_url }}) for student-facing work. This page is the English structure twin.

## Master idea

AI, augmented reality and virtual worlds change the fashion stage; authenticity, disclosure and data ownership are design problems, not small print.

## Learning outcomes

You will explain what AR try-on promises and who supplies the evidence, analyse an AI-model campaign in terms of labour, diversity and training data, place an image on a disclosure ladder and write its label, treat avatars and brand worlds as data and ownership systems, and declare a human–machine collaboration.

## Canvas task (10 points) and portfolio

1. **Explore an emerging technology.** One technology, one real sourced case (not the unit's cases), and the promise · proof · risk · disclosure card.
2. **Authenticity dilemma.** Place one of your own fashion images on the disclosure ladder and write the exact label you would publish. Local tools only, or none.

The [AI declaration guide]({{ '/ai-declaration/' | relative_url }}) applies to every portfolio entry. The EU AI Act ([Regulation (EU) 2024/1689](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}, art. 50) requires certain AI-generated content to be disclosed.
