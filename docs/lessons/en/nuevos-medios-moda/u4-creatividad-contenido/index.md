## Master idea

Brand-aligned content turns an idea into a situated, participatory and testable narrative system.

## Learning outcomes

You will turn a brand tension into an editorial idea, sequence three platform-native pieces, justify format and accessibility choices, and declare AI assistance for each submitted piece.

## Canvas proposal

Prepare a three-piece social-content proposal: audience and tension, editorial idea, format/action for each piece, visual attribution, accessibility choice, missing evidence, and an AI declaration. The [AI declaration guide]({{ '/ai-declaration/' | relative_url }}) applies to every portfolio entry.

## Campaign context

Use [The Or Foundation’s Kantamanto context](https://deadwhitemansclothes.org/intro){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} to test whether a visual story gives a local economy, labour and circulation enough context. Do not generalise local evidence into a global claim.
