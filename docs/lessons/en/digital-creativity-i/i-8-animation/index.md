## Why this unit exists — and what it honestly does not have

**Claim:** an animated image implies motion through a deliberately chosen sequence of stills, and the craft is deciding which stills to keep — not which effect or plug-in to add. This is craft discipline: **the evidence matrix's row for I.8 is `NONE`**, no source validates a teaching method for digital animation. Tool documentation grounds *how* any given animation tool works, labelled `[PLATFORM]`, never a citation.

The `img-002` epigraph's own paradox — a loop that implies motion is, frame by frame, a sequence of still images — is left here as a **reflective prompt**, not a graded debate: this unit carries 0 h debates, and inventing a graded slot to fill would misrepresent the actual allocation. Sit with the paradox in B1's reading; it resurfaces in B2's process note.
