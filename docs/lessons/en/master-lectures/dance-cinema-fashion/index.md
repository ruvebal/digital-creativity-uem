## A notable quote

> «Partimos de la situación corporal, de la existencia, del estar de pié, de caminar y, luego, en último lugar, del saltar y del danzar. Puesto que dar un paso es un acontecimiento grandioso, mientras que levantar una mano, mover un dedo, no lo son menos».  
> — [(Schlemmer 1987, 112)](#ref-schlemmer-1987) · *Course translation:* “We start from the bodily situation, from existence, from standing, from walking, and only last of all from leaping and dancing. For taking a step is a grand event — and raising a hand or moving a finger no less.”

Oskar Schlemmer wrote this while designing some of the most geometric stage costumes of his time. The cycle starts where he did: with a body taking one step, dressed, and seen.

## Why this cycle

Fashion borrows constantly from dance and film: the swirl of a skirt, the line of a pose, the cut of a red-carpet moment. This cycle asks you to look at those borrowings twice — first as **form** (what does the garment do to the moving body, and what does the camera let us see?), then as **power** (when a movement travels from one stage, country or body to another, who is credited, who is paid, who is made strange?).

It serves the official content *Fuentes y referencias compositivas en el diseño de moda* (I.7) of Digital Creativity I, and touches *Creación de imágenes digitales animadas* (I.8) in the animation prompt of Part 2. It adds no unit, deliverable or grade: each part ends in a two-exercise Lab whose traces go to your portfolio.

### Tools used in both parts

| Tool | Question | Part |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Movement map** | Does the garment drape, extend, tense or reorganise the body? | 1 |
| **Frame and cut** | What does the camera show of the dressed body, and what does it hide? | 1 |
| **Evidence ladder** | Analogy, quotation, exchange or documented influence? | 2 |
| **AI audit** | What does an image generator assume about a tradition? | 2 |

Two rules hold across the cycle: **looking alike is not evidence of influence**, and **a pose cannot tell you where a movement came from**.
