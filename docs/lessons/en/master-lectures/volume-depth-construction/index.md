## Learning aims

- Name Alberti’s window / picture plane as a construction law, not decoration.
- Distinguish psychophysiological space from mathematical space (Panofsky kit P1).
- Read central vanishing-point space (P5) as a *symbolic form* [(Panofsky 1991, 14–15)](#ref-panofsky-1993).
- Bridge: multi-view construction ≠ fit proof — return to I.6.

## Masterclass (six moves)

1. **Alberti’s window** — the picture plane as cut through the visual pyramid.  
2. **Visual pyramid** — rays, station point, and the plane that freezes them.  
3. **Aerial / atmospheric depth** — Leonardo-facing orientation (gap-honest if unvaulted).  
4. **Symbolic form** — perspective as cultural construction [(Panofsky 1991, 14)](#ref-panofsky-1993).  
5. **P1 vs P5** — psycho/math space vs central VP kit slides.  
6. **Multi-view ≠ fit** — P6 fashion multi-view still cannot certify waistband tension.
