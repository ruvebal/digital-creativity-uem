## Why this unit exists: a fashion edit is a sequence of consent-aware choices

**Claim:** a fashion edit is a sequence of *consent-aware* choices — what plays automatically, what needs a click, what has captions — before it is a sequence of cuts. This unit teaches that discipline alongside the cutting/pacing craft, deliberately, rather than treating accessibility as an afterthought pass at the end.

### Declared gap — full, not partial

The grounding matrix's row for this CONTENIDOS anchor is **NONE**: no coat in the vault addresses fashion-video-editing pedagogy, at any confidence, resolved or unresolved. ### The debate prompt

Should every fashion video default to muted / no-autoplay on a portfolio page, even if it costs "impact"?
