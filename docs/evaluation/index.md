# Evaluation

Assessment rationale and rubrics for Digital Creativity I–II. Official evaluation weights come from the UEM guía (see track pages). Rubrics align with B1/B2/B3 hand-in packages in each lesson.

## 0-10 Compatibility

All Digital Creativity rubrics use **0-10** local scores for Campus compatibility. Institutional percentages decide how much each activity weighs in the subject grade; they do not change the row-level scoring scale.

| Band | Score | Studio evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Insufficient | 0-4.9 | Missing deliverable, undeclared AI help, no reproducible process, or cannot explain the work |
| Minimum pass | 5-6.9 | Required artefacts present with basic technical and conceptual explanation |
| Competent | 7-8.9 | Complete process evidence, coherent visual decisions, and defensible iteration |
| Excellent | 9-10 | Reproducible workflow, precise critique, strong authorship, and mature technical execution |

## Shared AI And Oral Defence Scale

AI declarations, project critiques, and oral defences are graded with the same **0-10** compatibility bands. The assessment target is not polish alone: students must show what they made, what tools assisted them, what they verified, and what they can still explain without the tool present.
